Water Damage Restoration in Salem, OR FAQ

Do you work directly with insurance companies?

We work with most insurance companies and provide documentation to support your claim. We recommend contacting your insurer as soon as damage occurs, and we can coordinate with them on the repair scope.

How fast can you respond to water damage?

Water damage should be addressed as quickly as possible to limit mold risk and structural harm. Call or text (503) 999-9277 and we'll assess the situation and next steps.

Do you handle mold remediation?

Yes — mold remediation and prevention is part of our water damage restoration process, alongside structural drying, cleanup, and rebuild.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes — Andrade Construction LLC is licensed with the Oregon Construction Contractors Board (CCB #182464), bonded, and carries general liability insurance.
